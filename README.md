## About me

I'm a Master's student in Computer Science at Boston University, graduating in January 2027. I like taking a messy business problem and turning it into something that actually works, whether that's a multi-agent workflow, a data pipeline, or a model.

- 🤖 Most recently at **GEP Worldwide**, I built agentic workflows for enterprise procurement with LangGraph
- 📊 Before that, I spent two years at **Accenture** building SQL pipelines and data warehouse fixes for a global pharma client.
- 📄 Published at **FICC 2024** (IEEE, Berlin) for eye disease prediction from OCT scans.
- 🏆 Led a team of 3 to win two categories at **Evathon 2021** (University of Limerick).
